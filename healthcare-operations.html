Team Building

My leadership approach is simple but effective:

  • Empower people at all levels of the organization.
  • Communicate openly, honestly, and frequently.
  • Create a culture of collaboration, trust, and respect.
  • Establish a mentorship program to ensure the next generation of leaders are developed and ready to lead.
  • Deploy the use of SMART Goals so each teammate can drive their own career path.
  • Implement the Capability Maturity Model to keep creating capacity, consistency, and continuous improvement.
  • Celebrate success.
  • Grow AI literacy within the organization.
